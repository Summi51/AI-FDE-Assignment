# Sales bot: first quarter results

- **From:** Data and BI team with the external build partner
- **To:** Chief Digital Officer, Global Sales Head
- **Date:** 14 July 2025

## Summary

The sales bot went live on 17 March 2025 for India and the United Kingdom. It opened to all sales users on 2 June 2025. Usage is growing every week. Pilot territories show strong sales growth.

## How it works

- A user asks a question in the team chat tool or on the web page
- A language model turns the question into SQL
- The SQL runs on the warehouse tables
- The model writes the result back as a sentence and a table

## Adoption

- 93 users enabled
- Over 400 questions in the week of 23 June
- Most common topics: sales by territory, top distributors, overdue amounts and target achievement

## Business impact

| Group | Jan to Mar 2025 vs Apr to Jun 2025 sell-in |
|---|---|
| Pilot territories (India and UK) | +62% |
| All other territories | -45% |

Pilot territories grew strongly in the quarter after launch. Other territories declined. We recommend the bot for all regions.

## Next steps

- Add more languages
- Improve response time
