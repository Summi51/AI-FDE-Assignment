# Riklr call notes: first call with the commercial team

- **Date:** 22 September 2026
- **Attendees:** Global Sales Head and two Regional Sales Managers. One Sales Operations Analyst and Riklr
- **Written by:** Riklr

## What we heard

- Quarterly plans are built in spreadsheets by each territory manager. Regional managers review them in a monthly call
- Plans start from last year's numbers and the target. Then people adjust by feel
- Dashboards show what happened. Nobody trusts them for what will happen
- Sales and finance numbers differ every month. Meetings spend time on which number is right
- Territory managers decide where to go each week from memory and phone calls

## Jobs the team named

| Job | Who asked | What they do today |
|---|---|---|
| Which distributors to visit this week | Regional managers | Memory and phone calls |
| Where will the quarter land | Global Sales Head | Spreadsheet roll-up in the last two weeks of the quarter |
| Who is overdue and why | Regional managers | Finance aging report by email once a month |
| Which distributors or dealers stopped buying | Sales Operations | Ad hoc query to the BI team |
| Which scheme credits are pending | Territory managers | Phone call to the regional office |
| Which distributors are carrying stock that will come back | Regional managers | Distributor says "it's moving" |
