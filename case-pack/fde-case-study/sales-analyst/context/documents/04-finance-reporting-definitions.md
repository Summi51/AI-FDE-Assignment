# Commercial reporting policy: extract

- **Owner:** Finance
- **Version:** November 2025
- **Applies to:** Monthly close, board reporting, credit committee

## Net sales

Net sales is the reported revenue measure. It equals gross invoiced sales minus returns credited minus scheme and rebate credit notes.

- Gross invoiced sales are counted by invoice date
- Returns are counted by return date
- Scheme and rebate credits are counted by accrual date, whether issued or not. Cancelled credits are excluded
- Negative invoice lines are treated as returns
- All amounts convert to USD at the monthly average rate of the transaction month

## Exchange rates

- Treasury publishes daily rates on business days as local currency per USD
- The monthly average uses all rates published in the month
- The UK bills in GBP and Canada bills in CAD. Rates are applied per invoice currency, not per region

## Return rate

Returns credited in a period divided by gross sales in the same period, in USD

## Receivables

- Aging is reported at each month end per distributor
- Buckets: not due, 1 to 30 and 31 to 60 days past due. Then 61 to 90 and over 90 days past due
- Over 90 days is critical and goes to the credit committee
- Disputed amounts are flagged when a distributor withholds payment against a pending credit

## Scheme credits

- Schemes accrue at the end of each scheme period
- The credit note must be issued within 30 days of accrual
- Volume rebates and loyalty bonuses accrue quarterly

## Active distributor

A distributor with an invoice in the fiscal year or a non-zero balance at year end
