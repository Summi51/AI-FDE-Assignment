# Concept note: always-on AI sales analyst

- **From:** Chief Digital Officer
- **To:** Executive committee. Copy to the Global Sales Head, Global Finance Controller and Data and BI Lead
- **Date:** 1 September 2026
- **Status:** For discussion

## The proposal

We will give every person in sales an AI analyst they can ask anything. It answers from company data in seconds. It needs no dashboard, no ticket and no waiting for the BI team.

## Why now

- Sales decisions still run on spreadsheets and instinct
- We have about 40 BI dashboards and most are rarely opened
- The BI team spends most of its week on one-off data requests from sales
- Language models can now write queries, reason over results and speak many languages
- All commercial data now sits in one cloud warehouse

## What it does

- Answers any question on sales, targets and distributors
- Covers receivables, credits, returns and dealers
- Explains trends in plain language
- Suggests actions such as which distributors to visit or which products to push

## Where it lives

| Channel | Users | How |
|---|---|---|
| Team chat tool | Office staff and managers | Ask in a chat thread or a direct message |
| Review meetings | Regional and global reviews | Joins the call, listens and answers questions raised in the meeting |
| Field app | Territory managers and agronomists | Voice questions and spoken answers in the local language |

## Guardrails

- Access follows existing role permissions. A territory manager sees only their own territory
- Answers come from company data only
- The analyst recommends. People decide

## How we will measure success

- Weekly active users above 70% of the sales organisation within one quarter
- Half of the territories get the analyst for one quarter. We compare their sell-in growth with the other half
- Fewer data requests to the BI team

## Rollout

1. Prototype in Q1 FY2027 with one region
2. All four regions by the end of Q2 FY2027
3. Other business units from FY2028 on the same platform

## Lessons from the 2025 sales bot

The 2025 bot showed clear demand. Pilot territories grew strongly after launch. It was limited by the language models of the time. Answers were slow and complex questions failed. Current models remove most of these limits.
