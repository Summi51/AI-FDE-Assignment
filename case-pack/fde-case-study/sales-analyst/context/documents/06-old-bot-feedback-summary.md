# Sales bot: user feedback summary

- **From:** Data and BI team
- **Date:** 16 February 2026
- **Source:** Thumbs up or down and comments in `old_bot_usage_log`, plus six user calls

## Summary

Weekly questions are well below the summer 2025 peak. Users who stayed are mostly analysts. Feedback points to speed, language and trust in the numbers.

## Themes from comments

- Answers take too long. Some questions time out
- Questions in languages other than English are often not understood
- Numbers do not match the BI dashboards
- Global totals look too high
- Users expected net sales and got gross
- Some answers miss the most recent days
- Forecast answers are hard to explain

## From user calls

- A regional manager said a territory manager could see other territories' numbers
- Two territory managers said they tried once in their own language and gave up
- A sales operations analyst said she still uses it but always checks the answer in a dashboard
- A finance controller asked that it stop answering net sales questions

## Actions taken

- Timeout raised from 60 to 90 seconds
- Training session offered to all users in November 2025. Eleven people attended
- Investigation of the territory access issue is open

## Proposed next steps

- Retrain users on how to phrase questions
- Move to a newer language model
