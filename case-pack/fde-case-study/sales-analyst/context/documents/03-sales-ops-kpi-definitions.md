# Sales KPI handbook: extract

- **Owner:** Sales Operations
- **Version:** 3.2, April 2023. Return rate added August 2024
- **Applies to:** Targets, sales dashboards, incentive plan

## Sales

Sales is the value invoiced to distributors after on-invoice discount. Returns and credit notes do not reduce Sales. Territory teams do not control them, and they are reported by Finance.

- Date basis: order date
- Currency: the territory's billing currency. No conversion
- Source: `sell_in_orders.invoice_amount_local`

## Target achievement

Sales divided by target for the same territory and period. Targets are set per territory, fiscal quarter and product category.

## Return rate

Packs returned from a season's orders divided by packs sold in that season. Quantity based. A season is the main sell-in window for the region.

## Active distributor

A distributor with at least one order in the last 12 months

## Sell-through

Sales Operations does not own this metric. See the loyalty program reports.

## Quarter-end guidance

Territory managers should close any target gap with confirmed distributor orders before quarter end. Orders placed by the last day of the quarter count in that quarter.

## Incentive plan

Territory manager incentives pay on Sales against target. Payout starts at 90% achievement and caps at 130%.
