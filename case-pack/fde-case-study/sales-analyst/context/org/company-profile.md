# Company profile

## The company

- Global agri-inputs company: seeds, crop protection and biologicals
- Sells through distributors. Distributors sell to dealers and retailers. Dealers sell to growers
- This case covers the commercial business in four regions. Other business units are out of scope
- Fiscal year runs 1 October to 30 September. FY2026 closed on 30 September 2026
- Reporting currency is USD

## Regions

| Region | Hemisphere | Territories | Billing currency | Main sell-in months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North America | Northern | 8 (7 US, 1 Canada) | USD. CAD in Canada. | December to April |
| Brazil | Southern | 8 (4 South, 4 Center) | BRL | July to October. Smaller peak January to February. |
| Europe | Northern | 8 | EUR. GBP in the United Kingdom. | February to April and August to October |
| India | Northern | 10 | INR | May to July (kharif) and October to November (rabi) |

Seasons run on different calendars. A good quarter in India can be a quiet quarter in Brazil.

## The channel

| Level | Count | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Distributors | About 130 | Buy from the company on credit terms. Each sits in one territory. |
| Dealers and retailers | About 1,100 | Buy from distributors. Some are enrolled in the loyalty program. |
| Growers | Not tracked | Buy from dealers |

Payment terms differ by region. North America runs 60 days. Europe runs 90 days. India runs 75 days. Brazil runs about 150 days to match the harvest.

The loyalty program pays dealers a small bonus for each pack scanned at the point of sale to a grower. Enrollment varies by region: about three quarters of dealers in North America and about a quarter in India.

## How a quarter is planned today

1. Sales Operations sends each territory its target, set on gross sell-in in local currency
2. Territory managers build a plan in a spreadsheet: which distributors, which products, how much
3. Regional sales managers review the plans in a monthly meeting with dashboards on screen
4. Finance reports net sales in USD after month end. The two numbers rarely match
5. Near quarter end, territory managers push orders to close the gap to target
