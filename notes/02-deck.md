# Deck — 8 slides

Speaker notes under each slide. Numbers are from the warehouse extract of 30 September 2026, not from interview claims.

## 1. The ask, and the refusal

The CDO wants an AI analyst in chat, in meetings, and by voice, for every sales role.

We should not build that first.

The 2025 bot already tried "ask anything". People stopped trusting it.

## 2. The 2025 bot, from the log

9,125 questions. Peak week: 83 people.

57% answered. 889 hit the 90-second timeout. 1,630 of the answered questions got a thumbs down.

Marathi, Hindi and Portuguese were mostly "not understood".

The failures were rules: one service account, currencies added together, a third definition of sales.

## 3. The job that is already broken

A territory manager's Monday is a 9:30 call, then a finance overdue list on the messaging group.

He copies the red numbers into a notebook. He does not have the pending credit list. His distributor does.

Vidarbha, six distributors: 16 credits still accrued, INR 2.07 crore, oldest one 179 days.

## 4. Where the time goes

Not in a missing chatbot.

It goes in the phone call to the regional office: "which credits are pending, when will they come."

Every visit starts with ten minutes of that argument. Finance then calls about an invoice the distributor is holding on principle.

## 5. What we built

A Monday list for one territory manager.

Sorted by the real action: raise unissued credits before you chase collection.

Open a distributor and the brief shows pending credits, disputed overdue, and what is left to collect. Amounts stay in INR. The metric name is on the screen.

He cannot open another territory.

## 6. One distributor

Malana Agri Traders.

Unissued credits INR 95.6 lakh. Overdue INR 7.05 crore. Disputed INR 1.05 crore.

Do not open with "why have you not paid".

Scans fell 62% versus his own prior 8 weeks, and only 2 of 8 dealers are enrolled. Do not push another truck. Do not compare him with a distributor who scans more.

## 7. The return nobody "saw"

Sales called the Brazil South returns weather.

Feb–Apr 2026: 188,939 packs came back, from 18 distributors.

Scans in that sub-region had already fallen from 91,701 packs in October 2025 to 36,877 in January, while sell-in was still being pushed.

The flag is that pattern. It is not a weather forecast.

## 8. How we would prove it

Not weekly active users. The last bot had users.

On one territory, one quarter:

- Credits raised on Monday that were accrued and unissued. Today he finds these by phone.
- Days from accrual to issue. India's open credits already average 81 days.
- Trucks not pushed into a distributor already flagged. The baseline is those 188,939 packs.
- Wrong-territory answers: zero. Last time the filter lived in the prompt. It failed.
