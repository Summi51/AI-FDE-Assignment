# Video script — under 4 minutes

Record the deck, then switch to the prototype. Speak slowly. Cut a sentence before you rush.

## 0:00 — Problem (deck, slides 1–2)

The company asked for an AI analyst in chat, in meetings, and by voice.

They tried that in 2025. Nine thousand questions. In the busiest week, 83 people used it. Not hundreds. Half the answers were late, wrong, or not understood. Marathi and Hindi did not work. The bot added rupees to dollars. A territory manager could see another territory.

A better model does not fix that. The rules were wrong.

## 0:50 — The workflow (deck, slides 3–4)

The person this is for leaves home at seven. Monday at 9:30 he is on a bike, on a call, reading numbers from a notebook.

Finance sends an overdue list. He does not have the list of credits the company still owes. His distributor does. In Vidarbha that list is sixteen credits, two crore rupees, the oldest one open for 179 days.

Every visit starts with that argument. Then finance calls him about an invoice the distributor is holding until the credit note arrives.

## 1:35 — The prototype (switch to http://127.0.0.1:8765/)

This is his Monday list. Not a chatbot.

Malana is first because the overdue is already disputed against credits that were never issued. Vesorex is the same. Sumonia is at the bottom. Nothing to raise.

Open Malana.

Do not start with payment. Ninety-five lakh in credits is still accrued. One crore is already marked disputed. What is left to collect is a different number, and it is in rupees, not a mix of currencies. The sales figure is labelled. It is gross sell-in, Sales Operations' definition, not Finance's.

Scans at his dealers fell 62 percent against his own previous eight weeks. Only two of eight dealers are enrolled, so this is not compared with anyone else. The line says do not push another truck.

## 2:45 — Why that line exists (deck, slide 7)

Last season in Brazil South, 188,939 packs came back between February and April. The company called it weather.

Dealer scans had already dropped, from 91,000 packs in October to 37,000 in January, while the company was still shipping. The distributor said his dealers stopped reordering before the rain failed. Nobody asked him what left his warehouse.

## 3:20 — Close (deck, slide 8)

We would not measure weekly users. The last bot had users.

On one territory, for one quarter: credits raised on Monday that were sitting unissued. Days from accrual to a credit note. Trucks not pushed into a distributor already flagged. And zero answers from a territory he does not cover.

That is the prototype. Chat, meetings, and voice come after the number is one people will trust.
